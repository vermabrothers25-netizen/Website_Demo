FAQ

Common Questions

What sizes are available?
We offer Twin, Full, Queen, King, and California King. Each set includes a fitted sheet, flat sheet, and two pillowcases (one for Twin).
What's the difference between Cotton and Sateen?
Cotton sheets are crisp, cool, and breathable — great for hot sleepers. Sateen has a silky, smooth finish that feels more luxurious but is slightly warmer. Both are made from long-staple cotton fibers.
Do you offer bulk pricing for hotels?
Yes! We work with hotels, vacation rentals, and hospitality groups. Email bulk@whitethread.com with your requirements and we'll provide a custom quote.
How do I wash WhiteThread sheets?
Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, with mild detergent. Tumble dry low. Avoid bleach and high heat. Your sheets will get softer with every wash.
